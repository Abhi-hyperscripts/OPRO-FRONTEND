Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how Wisetrack Technologies ("we", "us") handles information in WorkWize, including the WorkWize QuickFix mobile app and the WorkWize web portal (together, the "Service"). The Service is a work-management tool that organisations, such as hotels and resorts, provide to their staff. Your organisation decides who gets an account and what you can see.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email or username, designation, role, department and team. Your organisation's administrator creates your account; the app has no public sign-up.
  • Work records: the jobs you create, assign, start or close, including the area, description, remarks, timestamps and who performed each step.
  • Photos: images you choose to attach to a job, taken with your camera or picked from your gallery. The app accesses the camera or photos only when you tap to add a photo.
  • Device information for notifications: a push notification token issued by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and the device type (Android or iOS), so we can notify you about jobs.
  • Sign-in data: a sign-in token stored on your device so you stay signed in. Your password is sent over an encrypted connection and is never stored on the device.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

How we use information

  • To provide the Service: show, create and update jobs for your organisation and team.
  • To send notifications about jobs that are assigned to you or your team.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent misuse and troubleshoot problems.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Sharing

  • Your organisation: work records and photos are visible to authorised users in your organisation, according to their roles.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications, and our hosting providers store data on our behalf. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • When you choose to share: if you use the WhatsApp share button, the job details you share are sent through WhatsApp under WhatsApp's own terms.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and the Service.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections, and access requires signing in. Access within your organisation is limited by role.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information and work records for as long as your organisation uses the Service, or as your organisation requires for its records. When you sign out of the app, it removes your notification token and sign-in data from the device.

To delete your account and the personal data linked to it, ask your organisation's WorkWize administrator, or email us at abhishekanand.ko@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my WorkWize account". We confirm and complete deletion within 30 days. Records your organisation must keep, such as completed job history, may be kept with your name removed.

Your choices

  • You can turn off notifications, camera or photo access at any time in your device settings.
  • You can ask to access or correct your information through your administrator or by email.

Children

The Service is meant for workplace use and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify users in the app or by email.
